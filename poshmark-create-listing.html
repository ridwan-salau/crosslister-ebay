    New With Tags Policy

    An item is New With Tags (NWT) if it is brand-new, unused, and has all of its original tags attached. All original packaging (box, dust bag, authenticity tags, etc) should be included with your item if applicable. Items that are new without tags and original packaging are not considered to be NWT.

    Photo Tips
    For best results

    • Place your tag on a dark background in a well-lit area.

    • Position the camera directly over the tag to include the barcode, brand, size, and price.

    • Make sure that all four corners of the tag and the barcode are captured.
